Core concepts
=============

ShakeLab organizes waveform data into three levels:

* **Record**: a continuous segment of samples and its metadata.
* **Stream**: multiple records belonging to the same waveform stream.
* **StreamCollection**: a collection of streams, such as several channels.

The waveform interfaces are being revised in the c-develop branch. Runnable
examples will be added after checking their signatures against that branch.
